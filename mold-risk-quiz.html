Straight answers

Mold Quiz FAQs

What this quiz is, what it is not, and what happens next.

Is this quiz a mold inspection?

No. This is a screening checklist built from the warning signs local remediation pros look for. Only an on-site inspection with moisture readings can confirm mold and find its source.

What should I do if my result is higher risk?

Do not disturb anything that looks like growth — breaking it up spreads spores through the home. Photograph what you see, and call to be routed to a local remediation pro for an inspection.

Do you collect my answers?

No. The quiz runs entirely in your browser: nothing is sent, stored, or shared. If you call, you are connected with a local provider — that is the only thing that ever leaves this page.

Does Water Damage Gwinnett perform mold inspections?

No. We are a referral and call-routing service, not a contractor. We connect you with independent local contractors who inspect, quote, and perform the work.
