# Water Damage Gwinnett — social playbook

How we post on Facebook, Instagram, Threads and Pinterest, and how social ties
into the site. RULES.md still wins on any conflict. Adopted Oct 26, 2026 from a
Muse research playbook after review: what was kept, changed or rejected is
recorded at the bottom so it doesn't come back unchecked.

**Profiles:** facebook.com/waterdamagegwinnett · instagram.com/waterdamagegwinnett ·
threads.com/@waterdamagegwinnett · pinterest.com/waterdamagegwinnett

## Ground rules for every post
- Connector framing: "we connect you with independent local restoration
  providers." Never "our team / crew / technicians," never "trusted" or
  "vetted," never a price, review, testimonial, rating, speed promise, 24/7
  claim (the county's own 24/7 line may be named as the county's), or
  "guaranteed."
- Any post with a call to action carries the site's disclosure in short form:
  "We may be paid a fee for calls we route to independent providers." (Spanish:
  "Es posible que nos paguen una tarifa por las llamadas que dirigimos a
  proveedores independientes.") Change "may be" to "are" when RULES.md 1.4 does.
- Practical and calm (RULES 8.7): no fear-based urgency ("before it's too
  late"), no disaster footage presented as local, no implied county affiliation
  or county seals (RULES 8.3).
- Images: real license-free photos (Pexels) or our own. Never AI-generated
  images. Never film or show a provider's job as our work.
- Never buy followers, likes, comments or reviews; no engagement pods.

## Where posts send people
- **Default destination is the matching site page,** not the phone number: the
  site carries the disclosure, the recording notice and the approved number.
- **Open question before putting the number in posts or turning on Facebook's
  "Call" button:** confirm the current call network allows social media as a
  traffic source (RULES 2.4–2.5 require approved sources). Until confirmed,
  posts and buttons link to the site. Marketcall does not accept organic
  social posts as a traffic source (RULES 9.12), so for Marketcall the answer
  is already no.
- Spanish posts link to the /es/ twin of the page.
- Tag every link with UTM parameters so GA4 shows what social brings:
  `?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<topic>`
  (platform = facebook, instagram, threads, pinterest; campaign = page topic,
  e.g. burst-pipe). The canonical tags keep these out of Google's index.

## Content pillars
1. **Educate (most posts):** shutoff valve location, first safe steps after a
   leak, freeze prep, sewer-backup "who to call" (county first), mold-moisture
   basics. Each post is built from the wording already on the matching page —
   no new claims invented for a caption.
2. **How it works:** what happens when you call, that we're a referral service
   and not the contractor or the county. Links to the "How this service works"
   page once it exists (RULES 8.2).
3. **Local:** name the Gwinnett towns we serve and link the city page; seasonal
   local risks (freeze, summer storms) tied to verified facts only.
4. **Call to action (no more than 1 in 5 posts):** "If you have active water
   damage in Gwinnett County, here's how to reach a local provider" + link + the
   disclosure line.

## Accessibility (added Oct 30, 2026)
- **Alt text on every image post and pin**, written by us, never the
  platform's automatic guess ("May be an image of body of water and text").
  Say what the image shows and include every word printed on it; about two
  sentences (~125 characters where possible). Don't paste the caption in as
  alt text. Spanish posts get Spanish alt text.
- **Videos:** on-screen text isn't read aloud, so the caption carries the
  video's key point in plain words. Any video with speech gets reviewed
  captions (Meta's caption tools, edited before posting).
- **Captions in sentence case.** No all-caps sentences in the caption text.
  A short all-caps headline on the graphic itself is fine, because the alt
  text carries its words.
- **Hashtags in capitalised words** (#WaterDamage, #GwinnettCounty), placed
  at the end of the caption, so screen readers can split the words. The local
  hashtag set stays, capitalised.
- **Emoji:** sparingly, never as bullets, never as the first character of
  the caption, never repeated in a row.
- These sit alongside the rules above (fee disclosure on CTA posts, no
  urgency, EPA wording for mold timing, phone hold); none of them relaxes
  those.
- Sources: [Mass.gov social media accessibility](https://accessibility.mass.gov/training/social),
  [CUNY: hashtags](https://guides.cuny.edu/accessibility/hashtags),
  [University of Houston: social media accessibility](https://www.uh.edu/marcom/guidelines-policies/social-media/accessibility/).

## Per platform
- **Pinterest (highest priority — search traffic that lasts):** 2:3 pins
  (1000×1500), keyword-rich title and description with the town name, linking
  to the exact page. Boards by topic and area, e.g. "Burst pipe first steps —
  Gwinnett County," "Freeze prep for Lawrenceville homes," plus Spanish boards
  for the /es/ pages.
- **Facebook:** about 80% helpful, 20% call to action. Short "what to check"
  videos or photo carousels. Answer questions with process, never time windows.
- **Instagram:** the same pieces adapted (new caption and cover, not a
  copy-paste), Reels for short how-tos, Stories polls ("Do you know where your
  main shutoff is?") with a link sticker to the page.
- **Threads:** short tips and replies to genuinely local conversations; no
  promotional replies in other people's threads.

## Cadence (a floor, not a growth promise)
Within the owner's limited time, batch once a week:
- Pinterest 3–5 pins/week
- Facebook 3/week
- Instagram 2–3/week (reuse Facebook pieces, adapted)
- Threads 2–3/week, as time allows

Review monthly: GA4 sessions by utm_source, calls in the network dashboard,
comments and messages — not follower counts.

## Seasonal calendar
- **Before the first hard freeze (November):** freeze prep, find the main
  shutoff, the first hour after a burst (RULES 8.1). Publish the site pages
  first, then the pins and posts that point to them.
- **Spring/summer storms:** roof and window leaks, flooded basement first
  steps, sewer backups after heavy rain.

## Reviewed and not adopted (from the Muse playbook)
- "Request a referral via our website form," Stories link to a "referral form":
  there is no form, by design; the site is inbound-call only (RULES 2, 8.6).
- "A professional in the network will contact you": not how it works; the
  caller is connected live. Describe the real process only.
- "Meet the referral team": there is no team to show; this would imply staff
  that doesn't exist.
- Instagram 3–5 posts/week plus daily Stories, Threads 3–5/week across both
  brands: more than the owner's time budget allows; the floors above replace it.
- Growth multipliers ("2–3×", "500% more calls"): unverified; never quoted.
